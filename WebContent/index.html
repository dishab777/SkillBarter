How Peer Skill Barter Works

1. Offer & Request

List skills you are willing to TEACH (e.g. Java, Web Development) and skills you want to LEARN (e.g. Python, SQL).

2. Reciprocal Matching

Our matching engine identifies compatible peer students who want what you teach, and teach what you want to learn.

3. Exchange & Rate

Connect with your peer, conduct your mutual learning sessions, mark the exchange as completed, and leave ratings.
